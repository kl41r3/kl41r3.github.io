I am an undergraduate at Duke Kunshan University majoring in Applied Mathematics and Computational Sciences (Mathematics track), advised by Prof. Luyao Zhang. In Fall 2026, I am spending a study-away semester at Duke University.

Research. I conduct interdisciplinary research at the intersection of computational and economic sciences, investigating the governance of AI-agent protocols and the economics of agentic commerce. I ask who sets the rules when AI agents communicate, transact, and coordinate, and who ends up holding influence over those rules. My work draws on generative AI, blockchain and decentralized systems (DAOs and Web3), information economics, and causal inference.

I am currently working on my capstone project: an operating-system-level silicon intelligence.

Leadership. I founded the DKU iOS Club, an active and innovative student-led tech community on campus, and now serve as its Senior Advisor. We organized AI Survival Class: From Idea to Product, one of the university's most popular workshop series, introducing students to cutting-edge AI technologies. The series attracted approximately 200 participants, over 10% of the student body.
